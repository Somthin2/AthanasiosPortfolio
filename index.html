Highlighted Work

I'm a cybersecurity analyst and bug bounty hunter based in Cyprus, relocating to Klagenfurt, Austria to pursue an MSc in AI & Cybersecurity at Alpen-Adria-Universität. I hold a First-Class BSc in Computer Science with a Minor in Mathematics from the University of Nicosia. My thesis — integrating ML-KEM-768 post-quantum key exchange into Signal Desktop — was accepted as an IEEE paper at CSNet'26 (10th Cyber Security in Networking Conference), co-authored with Harald Gjermundrød & Ioanna Dionysiou. A journal version has been submitted to MDPI.


Professionally I've run large-scale SIEM operations, built phishing simulation infrastructure from the ground up, and actively hunt real vulnerabilities across HackerOne and Intigriti programmes. I've completed all 52 PortSwigger Server-Side Vulnerabilities labs and ranked 241st out of 1,200 in a HackTheBox CTF.


I participated in an Erasmus+ programme in Sweden focused on information security, which sharpened my interest in the intersection of AI and cybersecurity. I'm fluent in Greek and English, currently learning German ahead of my move to Austria, and driven by finding the bugs that actually matter — and building the defences that stop the ones that get through.
